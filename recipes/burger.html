Burger

Description

A burger is a popular fast-food dish made with a juicy patty, fresh vegetables, cheese, and flavorful sauces, served between two soft burger buns.

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 beef patties
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients: Wash the vegetables, slice the tomato and onion, and separate the lettuce leaves.
  2. Season the patties: Sprinkle salt and pepper on both sides of the beef patties.
  3. Cook the patties: Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the patties for approximately 3–5 minutes per side, or until fully cooked.
  4. Add the cheese: Place a slice of cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking.
  5. Toast the buns: Cut the burger buns in half and lightly toast them in a pan.
  6. Add the sauces: Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the inside of each bun.
  7. Assemble the burger: Place the lettuce, tomato, onion, and beef patty on the bottom bun.
  8. Serve: Cover with the top bun and serve immediately while warm.

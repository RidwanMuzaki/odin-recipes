Telor Bali

Description

Telur Bali is a traditional Indonesian dish made with boiled eggs cooked in a spicy, savory red sauce. The sauce is made from chilies, shallots, garlic, tomatoes, and aromatic spices. It is commonly served with steamed white rice.

Ingredients

Main Ingredients

  • 6 boiled eggs, peeled
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 150 ml water

Spice Paste

  • 5 red chilies
  • 3 shallots
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon shrimp paste (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the eggs: Boil the eggs until fully cooked, peel them, and set aside. For extra flavor, lightly fry the eggs until the surface is golden.
  2. Prepare the spice paste: Blend the red chilies, shallots, garlic, tomatoes, and shrimp paste until smooth.
  3. Cook the spices: Heat the cooking oil in a pan. Add the spice paste and bay leaves. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until fragrant and the sauce thickens slightly.
  4. Season the sauce: Add salt, sugar, and water. Stir well and bring the sauce to a gentle simmer.
  5. Add the eggs: Place the boiled eggs into the sauce. Stir gently to coat the eggs evenly.
  6. Simmer: Cook for 5-10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the eggs absorb the flavors.
  7. Serve: Transfer the Telur Bali to a serving dish. Serve hot with steamed white rice.

Chicken Katsu

Description

Chicken Katsu is a Japanese dish made from boneless chicken coated in flour, egg, and breadcrumbs, then deep-fried until golden brown and crispy. It is commonly served with rice, fresh vegetables, and katsu sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs (preferably panko)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Cooking oil for frying
  • Katsu sauce for serving (optional)
  • Steamed rice and vegetables for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the chicken: Flatten the chicken breasts to an even thickness. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.
  2. Prepare the coating: Place the flour, beaten egg, and breadcrumbs in three separate bowls.
  3. Coat the chicken: Dip each chicken breast in flour, then egg, and finally breadcrumbs. Press gently to ensure the breadcrumbs stick.
  4. Heat the oil: Heat cooking oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Fry the chicken: Fry the chicken for approximately 4–6 minutes per side, depending on its thickness, until golden brown and fully cooked. The internal temperature should reach 74°C (165°F).
  6. Drain and slice: Remove the chicken and place it on a paper towel or wire rack to drain excess oil. Let it rest briefly, then slice into strips.
  7. Serve: Serve hot with steamed rice, vegetables, and katsu sauce.
